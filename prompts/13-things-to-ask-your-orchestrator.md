# 13: Things to ask your Orchestrator

The last step is yours. Pick one line below, paste it into your Orchestrator's chat, and watch what it does. These are written the way a person talks. You do not need clever wording.

What to watch for:
- The Orchestrator says **which bot** it hands the job to (Email, Calendar, Messaging and so on).
- It reads first and **asks before it sends, deletes, unsubscribes or saves anything**.
- After any request, you can ask: **Which bots did you use, and what did each one do?**

## Email

- Check my inbox and tell me what needs a reply today. Don't send anything.
- Find the newsletters and junk I never open. List them and ask me before you unsubscribe from anything.
- Summarize my unread email from the last 3 days in 10 lines.
- Read my last 30 sent emails and learn how I write. Make a voice card for writing to one person and another for writing to a group. Show me both.
- Draft replies to the 3 emails that matter most, in my email voice. Show me, don't send.

## Messages

- Summarize my WhatsApp and iMessage chats from this week. Who is waiting on me?
- Read my last 50 sent messages and learn how I text. Make voice cards for a close friend, a work contact, a small group and a big group. Show me.
- Draft replies to the 3 people who have waited longest. Show me, don't send.

## Calendar and tasks

- What's on my plate this week?
- Find two free hours this week for focused work and suggest when.
- Every morning at 8, give me a short briefing: calendar, tasks, anything urgent.
- Turn my last 5 emails into tasks and put them in one list.

## Your team

- I want to [plan trips / track my bookkeeping / keep my reading list]. Ask the Factory to build a new bot for it. Show me its instructions first and don't save it until I say OK.
- Which bot would you give [a task of your own] to, and why? Don't do it yet.

## Good to know

- Email, messaging, one person and a big group each get their own voice card. The Email and Messaging bots check every draft against the right card and run the humanizer on it, so it sounds like you and not like an AI.
- Your voice cards stay inside your bots. They never go to GitHub. Only the Code bot touches GitHub.
- Anything that sends, deletes or unsubscribes needs your OK first. If a bot skips that, say "stop" and report it.
- New bots from the Factory follow the same rules: one job each, ask before acting, no code unless it is the Code bot.
