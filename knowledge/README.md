# Knowledge

This folder is where your own notes, project summaries and exports go.

**Only put things here in your PRIVATE `my-agents` copy.** Never put your own knowledge into the public starter pack.

Suggested layout:

```
knowledge/
  INVENTORY.md          your list of projects, GPTs and skills, with tiers
  <project-name>/
    handoff.md          what it is, instructions, decisions, next steps
skills/
  <skill-name>/         a copy of each of your own Claude skills
```

Raw exports (the zip files from Claude and ChatGPT) stay on your computer. They are large and private. Only the short notes go to GitHub.

Start with `HOW-TO-EXPORT.md`. Then use `prompts/06-inventory-my-projects.md`, `07-distill-my-exports.md` and `08-copy-my-skills.md`. Use `prompts/03-knowledge-dump-chatgpt.md` or `03-knowledge-dump-claude.md` to fill gaps for your most important projects, and `prompts/04-push-dump-to-github.md` to save everything.
